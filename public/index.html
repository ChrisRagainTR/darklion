Before you get started

A few good questions.

Have a question about your firm?
Talk to our team.

Who is DarkLion for?

DarkLion is for accounting and tax firms that want client records, documents, conversations, and work tracking in one place. Your firm subscribes directly to DarkLion. No separate software subscription is required.

How do we get started?

DarkLion opens to new firms in 2027. Existing staff and clients can use their separate login links at the top of this page.

How does the pricing work?

The monthly price is $19 for the firm plus $5 for each enabled staff user. Five staff users total $44 per month. Client portal accounts are included. Your subscription is with DarkLion; optional services and final usage allowances are confirmed during setup.

Can we bring our existing clients and documents?

Talk with our team about your current system, available exports, document volume, and setup needs. We will review the migration approach with you. A one-click import from every practice platform is not currently offered.

What will onboarding look like?

Set up your firm workspace and subscription, invite staff, bring over client records and documents, choose your workflows, and invite clients. Workspace setup includes a contact import from a CSV file. For help with a larger migration, email support@darklion.ai.

Do clients need a paid account?

Your firm pays for DarkLion. Invited clients use the client portal with no additional client account charge. Staff use Staff login, and clients use Client login.

Is there a mobile app?

The web client portal is an existing part of DarkLion. A public DarkLion mobile app is planned; availability and supported platforms will be announced after release testing and distribution approval.

What does the AI do?

AI assists with conversation summaries and supported tax-document extraction. Staff review the original information and remain responsible for decisions. Routine assistance is part of the offering; usage allowances are confirmed during setup.

Which features can our firm use?

DarkLion brings together client records, pipelines, portal messaging, documents, proposals, organizers, tax delivery, and AI assistance. Our team will confirm supported services and integrations during setup. The illustrations on this page use fictional data and simplify the interface.

Are tax signatures and texting included?

Texting and paid identity-verification or signature services are separate from the base price. Supported workflows, provider requirements, availability, and fees will be confirmed before your firm uses those services.
